# ABTalks Redesign — Hackathon Demo Video Script

> **Target Duration:** 2:30 – 3:00 minutes  
> **Recommended Resolution:** 1080p (Mobile Viewport 390px centered or split-screen)  
> **Presenter:** Pavithra (Team 404 Found Us)  
> **Live PDF Script Page:** [`https://abtalksredesign.vercel.app/script`](https://abtalksredesign.vercel.app/script)

## 🎬 Video Production Checklist Before Recording

1. Open `https://abtalksredesign.vercel.app` in Chrome.
2. Press `F12` → Toggle Device Toolbar → Select **iPhone 12 / 13 / 14 (390px width)** or record fullscreen desktop.
3. Ensure microphone audio is crisp.
4. Have 2 sample URLs ready to paste:
   - `https://github.com/Sameer8549/AB-TALKS`
   - `https://linkedin.com/posts/abtalks-day12-demo`

## 📜 Scene-by-Scene Script & Action Guide

### SCENE 1: The Hook & The Problem (0:00 – 0:30)

| Time | On-Screen Action | Voiceover Script (What to Say) |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00 - 0:10** | Open `https://abtalksredesign.vercel.app/` (Landing Page). Hero section visible. | *"Hey judges! I’m Pavithra, member of Team 404 Found Us. Today, we’re presenting our redesign of **ABTalks** — the platform where Indian college developers build a 60-day coding habit."* |
| **0:10 - 0:22** | Slowly scroll down through **How It Works** and **Track Selector**. Toggle Light/Dark mode once. | *"Most students use ABTalks late at night on their phones — exhausted after college, trying to keep a habit alive. The original product mechanics worked, but the UI was raw and unforgiving."* |
| **0:22 - 0:30** | Click **"Join the Challenge"** button on landing page. | *"We reimagined ABTalks from the ground up — building an interface that adapts to the student instead of demanding discipline from them."* |

---

### SCENE 2: The Core Thesis & The StreakChain (0:30 – 1:05)

| Time | On-Screen Action | Voiceover Script (What to Say) |
|---|---|---|
| **0:30 - 0:45** | You land on `/dashboard` in **Normal State** (Arjun Mehta profile). Hover over the **StreakChain** links. | *"Welcome to the Student Command Center. Our signature feature is the **StreakChain** — not a generic contribution grid, but a physicalized chain of 60 links that glow, lock, crack, and repair."* |
| **0:45 - 1:05** | Point out the late-night copy, midnight countdown timer, and personal-best streak nudge. | *"Our design thesis is simple: **the chain knows what time it is, and it knows you’re tired.** At 11 PM, the UI copy softens. As midnight approaches, a live countdown creates urgency. And when you’re near your personal best, a quiet nudge keeps you moving."* |

---

### SCENE 3: Edge Cases & Recovery Grace (1:05 – 1:45)

| Time | On-Screen Action | Voiceover Script (What to Say) |
|---|---|---|
| **1:05 - 1:20** | Click **"Missed"** on the floating Preview State Switcher. The screen transitions to **Rahul Nair** with a red Recovery Banner. | *"Most streak apps punish failure instantly. Miss one day, and your 30-day streak resets to zero — which is when 80% of students quit forever. On ABTalks, a missed day triggers **Recovery Grace**."* |
| **1:20 - 1:35** | Click **"Repair Now"**. Paste GitHub & LinkedIn URLs into the repair form, then click **"Repair Day 3"**. | *"You get a 24-hour grace window to repair the fracture. When you submit late, we don't hide the mistake — we seal the link with a **Gold Seam**. The scar becomes proof of your resilience."* |
| **1:35 - 1:45** | Click **"New"** on the switcher to show **Priya Sharma** (Day 1). | *"We also designed every edge case with care — including brand-new students on Day 1 with zero streak, ensuring zero broken layouts."* |

## 💡 Top Recording Tips for Judges

1. **Speak clearly & confidently**: Use enthusiasm during the Recovery Grace and ProofCard sections!
2. **Mouse cursor movement**: Keep cursor movements smooth and intentional.
3. **Download demo**: When clicking "Download PDF Certificate", let the native print window show for 2 seconds so judges see the official gold certificate layout!
