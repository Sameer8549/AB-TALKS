# ABTalks — Product Narrative

## The Unifying Idea

ABTalks isn't a habit tracker that demands rigid discipline — it's a system that reads context. The chain metaphor is the product's spine: every day you build adds a link, and the platform adapts to protect that chain rather than punish you for missing it. Late at night, copy softens. Near midnight, urgency rises via a live countdown. When you're close to your personal best, the app nudges quietly. A missed day opens a 24-hour Recovery Grace window — not a badge of shame, but a chance to repair the crack so it becomes a gold seam, not a break. A completed day closes as shareable proof on GitHub and LinkedIn — the chain's real purpose. The product treats every student as someone in a different state each day (tired, on a streak, recovering) and responds accordingly, rather than treating every day identically.

## Core Concepts

| Concept | What it means in the product |
|---|---|
| **StreakChain** | The 60-day visual chain — completed days are solid links, recovered days show a gold seam, missed days crack |
| **Recovery Grace** | A 24-hour window to submit late proof; the crack repairs rather than breaks the chain permanently |
| **Time-aware copy** | UI language softens late at night, grows urgent near midnight, nudges quietly near a personal best |
| **Live countdown** | Real-time HH:MM countdown to midnight in the today's task card — urgency without shame |
| **Proof card** | A shareable record of a completed day with GitHub + LinkedIn links — the chain's output, not just its progress |

## For the README

> ABTalks is a 60-day coding challenge platform for Indian college students built around a single idea: the chain knows what time it is. Rather than treating every day identically, the platform adapts to context — softening copy late at night, surfacing a live countdown near midnight, nudging students close to their personal best, and offering a Recovery Grace window to repair a missed day rather than shame it into a permanent failure. The chain is the metaphor and the mechanic: a completed day adds a solid link, a recovered day shows a gold seam, and sixty days of proof close as a shareable GitHub + LinkedIn record.
